Attendance:

Much like other purchases—you will find yourself responsible for the quality of your experience. My responsibility is to many students—I expect that if you want to know what this whole typography thing is about, you’re going to show up on time—all the time. If you think this might not be the right time for this class consider doing this another time. If you are found absent more than twice you will be ineligible for an A grade. More than 3 times you would be ineligible for a C.

At Recitations:

The LA will be responsible for delivering a synopsis of the course. You will receive a handout. You will pick up an assignment that you can perform during that period. Sign and place the assignment in the folder on your way out of the room.

Each recitation assignment is also an attendance mechanism.

If the recitation has a reading and discussion assignment. This Happens during the session and the text is read out loud with everyone present.

Each person reads one or two paragraphs.

Each person writes a 2 sentence synopsis.

by hand.

Each recitation has a practice which you can participate with as long as you please.

If it is a quiz the quiz can rerefer to any source. All quizzes are to be handed in before you leave.

The work you make must be handed in before you leave the room.

There are no late assignments for the recitations don’t ask for extensions due to absence.

10 Assignments will be accepted of a possible 15 assignments during the semester.
