MAJOR ASSIGNMENT OF THE COURSE · 2

NUMBERS and Statistics

MA TWO · DUE 10/4

Pull 1 Statistic from each of the following:

Fin Tech - Financial Times

(Online or Newspaper. Provide citation)

Op Ed. - New York Times

(Online or Newspaper. Provide citation)

Sports - ESPN

(TV, Provide photo citation)

Produce (3) 4 Slide Decks to sell any aspect of the research. Recreate a branded template for the author of each deck. Each deck is from a different author position. Pro/Con/Neutral.

This is an assignment at the intersection of speculative futures and storytelling using—more or less—accepted forms of source material and retold and cited for the purposes of promoting a favorable/unfavorable circumstance and the consequences that are in the truth of this ‘fact’. Numbers are central to this work. There must be a number story.

Use AI at your own risk. AI errors are common, if the AI errors are present in your finished work. Your final grade for the assignment will drop by a full letter grade.

EXAMPLE:

A 4 Slide deck produced from a piece of information found in an ESPN report about the inbound trend of ‘AthleisureWear’. The statistic might support investment in a ‘breathable textile startup’. The author might be a tech/textile company selling the investment opportunity. Located in Jaipur, Northern India. With more than one technical fabric in its portfolio.

When complete there will be 3 separate decks.

All narratives are factual.

There must be evidence to support any inference.

Upload the work to canvas.
